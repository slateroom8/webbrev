In cities across Canada, the three restaurants worth knowing.

Brévant is a selective guide to dining in Canada. In every city we cover, we choose three restaurants and no more — the ones genuinely worth knowing, whether they opened last season or a decade ago. There is no ranking and no score; the three simply stand together. It is a short list by design, for anyone who would rather be sent somewhere good than left to sift through everything a city offers.
