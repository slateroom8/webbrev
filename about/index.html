About Brévant

Brévant is a selective guide to dining across Canada. In each city, we choose only three restaurants.

Rather than attempting to catalogue every good restaurant in a city, Brévant makes a smaller and more demanding commitment: three names, and no more. There is deliberately no ranking among them. They are simply the three Brévant selections for that city.

Selections weigh the cooking, its originality, its consistency over time, a restaurant's sense of place, and the whole of the experience it offers. Long-standing institutions may appear alongside restaurants that opened last year. What a place costs, how it is decorated, and how many awards it has gathered are not the point; whether it is worth knowing is.

The selections are not ranked, and they are not scored. There is no points system and no formula. Inclusion itself is the distinction.

Restaurants cannot purchase their way into Brévant. Nothing here is paid, sponsored, or arranged. The guide will change over time as restaurants evolve, as new rooms open, and as others close — a city's three are not fixed forever, only chosen carefully for now.
