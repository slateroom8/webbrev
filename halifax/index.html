The three Brévant selections

Halifax

An Atlantic city whose kitchens have caught up with its harbour, told in three rooms.

Mystic

Queen's Marque

Mystic juts out over Halifax Harbour in a glass-wrapped room in the Queen's Marque district, and Malcolm Campbell cooks a tasting menu built almost entirely from what Atlantic Canada gives up — land, sea and the wild edges between. The signature menus run eight courses of fluid, hyper-seasonal cooking: an Acadian caviar course of smoked egg yolk and wild sea flora, a lobster raviolo lifted with locally grown saffron, halibut with smoked mussels and pickled leek. Presentation is meticulous, but the point is provenance more than polish. It is the most ambitious dining room the city has, and it treats Nova Scotia's bounty as a subject worth studying, not just serving.

Tribute

Cunard Residences

Tribute defines itself not by a cuisine but by a place — Nova Scotia — and cooks it over live fire in an open kitchen on the waterfront. Colin Bebbington, a Halifax native who trained in Michelin-starred kitchens in Chicago and London before coming home, runs a forty-seat room offering a chef's tasting menu alongside à la carte. The cooking leans on wood and charcoal, handmade pasta, and seafood and meat from Nova Scotian producers, with menus that shift as the season turns. It is polished but energetic, the kind of place that gets loud on a good night, and since opening in late 2024 it has become one of the reasons to plan a trip around the harbour.

Bar Kismet

North End

Bar Kismet started as a seafood-and-natural-wine bar and quietly became one of the best kitchens in the Maritimes. Annie Brace-Lavoie and Jenner Cormier, who met at Toronto's Bar Raval, run a small, warm room on Agricola Street where the menus change weekly around what the season offers: raw scallop, tuna tartare, handmade pasta, plates built for sharing. The drinks are the other half of the appeal — an astute natural-wine list and cocktails whose flavour profiles are written out, half-seriously, as haiku. It is relaxed and local in feel and serious in execution, a neighbourhood restaurant that happens to cook at a level the neighbourhood is lucky to have.
